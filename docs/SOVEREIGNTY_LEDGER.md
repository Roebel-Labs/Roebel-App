# Sovereignty ledger

Status: 2026-11-17, branch `feat/sovsignup-4-backup-file`. Spec: [`superpowers/specs/2026-11-17-sovereign-signup-design.md`](superpowers/specs/2026-11-17-sovereign-signup-design.md) section 9.

This is an honest list of what a person controls and what still depends on a vendor or on us, for each of the three ways to sign in.
It is a document, not app copy, so it names vendors on purpose. **App copy and marketing must not claim more independence than this ledger supports** (see "What we may say in the app").

How to read it: every table has the same nine rows. "Depends on" names the party. The next two columns say what happens to the person if that party disappears, and if it turns hostile. Every fact was checked against the code named in brackets; what could not be checked says "not verified" and why.

Shared facts for all paths: the account is a Safe 1.4.1 on Gnosis chain 100 ([`lib/passkey/constants.ts`](../apps/expo/lib/passkey/constants.ts)). The Bürgerkarte (CitizenNFTv2) and the Röbel Münzen live on that chain, not on our servers. For people who signed in with thirdweb before, the citizen NFT stays on their original thirdweb smart account and the Safe is a co-admin of it (see the third path).

## Passkey

Default sign-up path. The account is a Safe owned by a passkey (P-256, checked on chain). Optional: Wiederherstellungswörter (guardians with a 3-day delay).

| Row | Depends on | If that party disappears | If that party turns hostile |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Key custody | The device's passkey provider: Apple iCloud Keychain or Google Password Manager. The passkey is created and used through the platform for the relying-party domain `id.ortis.app` and syncs between the person's devices through that keychain unless the person adds the Wiederherstellungswörter [`lib/passkey/webauthn.ts`, `PASSKEY_RP_ID` in `lib/passkey/constants.ts`]. Also on the domain `id.ortis.app` (served by our `ortis-id` Fly app) and its association files `apple-app-site-association` and `assetlinks.json` [`apps/roebel-id/src/well-known/app-associations.ts`]. | Keychain provider gone or account closed: the passkey is lost unless another device still holds it or the person has the Wiederherstellungswörter (recovery then takes 3 days). If our domain or its association files go away, the platform stops offering the existing passkeys to the app (iOS and Android check the file before they release a credential). The account itself is untouched and recoverable through guardians. Nothing in the repo recovers a lost domain automatically. Not verified: platform caching behaviour after the file disappears. | The provider can lock the person out of the keychain, not move the account: it never holds a key it could sign with for others. We control the domain: we could stop vouching for the app, which stops sign-in with the existing passkey but cannot move funds or the Bürgerkarte. Recovery words and guardians still work. |
| Key backup | Our server (Supabase) holds ciphertext of the voting (MACI), Nostr and salt keys, wrapped under the passkey PRF and, if set up, under a key derived from the recovery words (slots `maci`, `nostr`, `salt`, `*_w`, `wvk`) [`lib/passkey/key-backup.ts`, `lib/passkey/words-vault.ts`, `apps/web/src/lib/passkey/key-backup-store-supabase.ts`]. The server holds ciphertext only. | Backup lost and the passkey or words lost too: the voting, Nostr and salt keys are gone (a new set is created; old votes stay as they were cast, a vote not yet tallied cannot be changed). The account and its funds are not affected. With the passkey or the words, the keys open on a new phone while the backup exists. After a guardian recovery to a new key, only the copies wrapped under the words survive; those wrapped under the old passkey cannot be opened any more (known residual, plan 3 open question 4). | We can delete or withhold ciphertext, so the person loses those three keys. We cannot read it: opening needs the passkey PRF or the words. We could serve an old blob; the app unwraps and uses only blobs that authenticate. Not verified: behaviour on a deliberately swapped blob. |
| Recovery | The chain: guardians in the Candide SocialRecoveryModule `0x38275826…541c` with a 3-day delay [`lib/passkey/constants.ts`]. Words are 5 guardian addresses derived from the phrase, not instant control [`lib/passkey/words-recovery.ts`]. The optional "E-Mail oder Google als Helfer" uses thirdweb for the helper's login [`lib/passkey/recovery-helper-runtime.ts`]. Warnings by email (Resend, konto@roebel.app), by push (Expo push service) and our scheduled job on Vercel [`apps/web/src/lib/passkey/recovery-alerts.ts`, `apps/web/vercel.json`]. | Contract: immutable, no operator. Our warnings and the in-app banner stop: a recovery started by someone else is then found only by the person checking in the app, and the 3-day window could pass unseen. thirdweb gone: helpers set up with e-mail or Google can no longer sign in, the other guardians and the words still work. Resend or Expo push gone: the other warning channel still works if it was set up. | The module cannot be changed by us. Someone who holds enough guardians or the words can start a recovery; the owner can cancel it for 3 days, and warnings only help if they arrive. We could suppress warnings (the email, the push, the banner). We could not move the account ourselves. thirdweb could refuse the helper login, so that helper cannot confirm. |
| Gas | Our paymaster and sponsor route: `POST /api/passkey/sponsor` signs for the dedicated paymaster (env `PASSKEY_PAYMASTER_ADDRESS`; the production paymaster `0x11ed03Db…` must not sponsor passkey operations) with daily budgets [`apps/web/src/lib/passkey/sponsor-handler.ts`]. | The account still exists and its owner can pay gas in xDAI and send transactions with any Safe-compatible tool, for a passkey owner with a tool that can sign with the passkey, which the app does not offer. In the app nothing can be done until sponsoring returns. | We could refuse to sponsor, or sponsor only some people, within the rules the handler enforces (budgets, who may be sponsored). That is an interruption. The sponsor signer only authorises gas; it is not an owner and cannot sign for the Safe. Not verified: who controls the paymaster's owner Safe in production (set by the owner of the deployment). |
| Bundler | Our same-origin proxy `/api/bundler` forwarding to the operator's bundler URL in the server env `GNOSIS_BUNDLER_RPC_URL`; the app's default bundler URL is `https://www.roebel.app/api/bundler` [`apps/web/src/app/api/bundler/route.ts`, `PASSKEY_BUNDLER_URL` in `lib/passkey/constants.ts`]. The example in the proxy names Pimlico; not verified which provider is configured in production (an environment value, not in the repo). | Our proxy or its upstream down: sending stops in the app. Any other ERC-4337 EntryPoint v0.7 bundler can submit the same operations; a person can also use an own bundler by setting the app's variable (not exposed in the UI). | A bundler can delay or drop an operation and see its contents (public on chain anyway). It cannot change a signed operation. Not verified: that nothing else is logged upstream. |
| RPC | Default `https://gnosis-rpc.publicnode.com`, replaceable through `EXPO_PUBLIC_GNOSIS_RPC_URL` [`lib/passkey/userop.ts`, `DEFAULT_GNOSIS_RPC_URL`]. Run by a third party (PublicNode), not by us. | The app cannot read the chain until another RPC is configured (needs an app update or an env at build). The chain and the account are unaffected. | An RPC could return wrong data, or log the person's IP address and queries. Signed operations are checked on chain; a wrong read could mislead the screen, not move funds. |
| App distribution | The Apple App Store and Google Play to install; Expo (EAS Update) for over-the-air updates [`apps/expo/app.config.ts`, `updates.url`]. | Removed from a store: existing installs keep working; no new installs or store updates. EAS Update gone: no OTA; store builds still ship. The account works in any wallet that can sign with the passkey or the words (the passkey owner needs a tool that supports it). | A store can remove the app or refuse updates, which is an interruption. A bad update through our own channels is our responsibility: updates must never end a session, and each store build is tested on every sign-in path (`docs/STORE_BUILD_UPDATE_TEST.md`). |
| Database | Supabase (Postgres): profiles, feed, conversation registry, key-backup ciphertext, warning-email store, push tokens [`apps/web/src/lib/passkey/key-backup-store-supabase.ts`]. | Profiles, feed, sponsoring state and the key backup stop. The account, its Bürgerkarte, its Röbel Münzen and its guardians on Gnosis do not. | We (or Supabase) could delete or alter rows: the person loses profile content and the key backup. The chain state and the person's keys are not affected. |
| Hosting | Vercel (website, API routes incl. sponsor, bundler proxy, key backup, alerts), Fly (`ortis-id` identity domain, MACI coordinator). | Sponsoring, backup reads and alerts stop; passkey sign-in stops if `id.ortis.app` is gone (see Key custody). The account stays on chain. | We could take the services down or change them: an interruption, not a seizure. |

## 12 Wörter

Expert path. The account is a Safe with one ECDSA owner. The 12 words are a standard BIP-39 phrase (m/44'/60'/0'/0/0), so they work in any wallet. Optional: Wiederherstellungswörter (guardians).

| Row | Depends on | If that party disappears | If that party turns hostile |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Key custody | The phone's secure store, device-only (`WHEN_UNLOCKED_THIS_DEVICE_ONLY`), read only after biometrics (`requireAuthentication`). Nothing goes into a cloud keychain or a device backup [`lib/passkey/phrase-store.ts`]. Not stored by us. The person's own copy of the 12 words (paper) or the encrypted backup file (`docs/ACCOUNT_BACKUP_FILE_FORMAT.md`, standalone decrypt script). | Phone lost or secure store invalidated (fingerprints changed): the person needs the written words or the backup file plus its password. Without them the account is gone, except through guardians. No third party is involved. | Nobody else holds the key. An app update that wipes the phrase would be our bug, so updates are tested on this path (spec 5.9). A phone without biometrics cannot use this path (the app refuses it). |
| Key backup | Our server (Supabase) holds ciphertext of the voting, Nostr and salt keys, wrapped under the key derived from the phrase [`lib/passkey/key-backup.ts`]. | As on the passkey path: keys not in the backup are lost, the account is not. A guardian recovery to a new phrase loses the keys wrapped under the old phrase (known residual, plan 3 open question 4). | We can delete ciphertext, not read it. |
| Recovery | The words themselves; guardians (Candide SocialRecoveryModule, 3-day delay); the encrypted backup file. Same module, warnings and vendors as on the passkey path (Resend, Expo push, our job on Vercel; thirdweb for "E-Mail oder Google als Helfer") [`lib/passkey/words-guardians.ts`, `recovery-alerts.ts`]. | As on the passkey path. | As on the passkey path. |
| Gas | Our sponsor route, the same as for passkey accounts, with the ECDSA owner variant (`ecdsaOwner` in the request) [`sponsor-handler.ts`]. | The owner can pay gas in xDAI and sign with any wallet that accepts the 12 words. The app does not offer paying gas itself; any Safe-compatible tool does. | We could refuse to sponsor: an interruption, not a seizure. |
| Bundler | Same as the passkey path: our `/api/bundler` proxy and its upstream. | As on the passkey path. A wallet with its own bundler or plain transactions works without. | As on the passkey path. |
| RPC | Same as the passkey path: PublicNode by default [`lib/passkey/userop.ts`]. | As on the passkey path. | As on the passkey path. |
| App distribution | Same as the passkey path: the two stores and EAS Update. The words and the backup file work without the app: any wallet with the 12 words, or the standalone script. | The account is still reachable with another wallet; the app is not needed to hold the account. | As on the passkey path. |
| Database | Supabase, same as the passkey path. | Same as the passkey path: the account does not depend on it. | Same as the passkey path. |
| Hosting | Vercel and Fly, same as the passkey path; sign-in does not need `id.ortis.app` (no passkey). | Sponsoring, backup and alerts stop. The account stays reachable. | An interruption, not a seizure. |

## Google, Apple oder E-Mail

The earlier way in. The key is held by the sign-in provider (thirdweb in-app wallet) and the account is a thirdweb smart account on Gnosis. This path is not independent: the person depends on thirdweb until they choose "Unabhängig werden", which makes the passkey Safe the only administrator of the account (irreversible, needs guardians; preview-gated) [`lib/passkey/detach.ts`, `app/settings/passkey-detach.tsx`].

| Row | Depends on | If that party disappears | If that party turns hostile |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Key custody | thirdweb holds the key (in-app wallet, social or e-mail login) until "Unabhängig werden". With a passkey Safe added as co-admin, the passkey is a second way, not the only one. | thirdweb gone before the person went independent: the person can still sign in with the passkey Safe if one was added; if not, the account is at risk. After "Unabhängig werden", thirdweb is out of the picture. | thirdweb (or a stolen login) could act as an admin until "Unabhängig werden". That is exactly why this path is not called independent. |
| Key backup | thirdweb derives the voting, Nostr and salt keys from a deterministic signature; passkey accounts store them as ciphertext on our server (Supabase) [`lib/passkey/key-backup.ts`]. | thirdweb gone: the derivation cannot be repeated; the keys exist only where a backup was written. | thirdweb could withhold the derivation signature. |
| Recovery | The sign-in provider's login (Google, Apple, e-mail) while on thirdweb. After a passkey Safe is added, guardians with the 3-day module (same as the other paths). | The provider's account recovery decides; ours only after the passkey Safe exists. | The provider could lock the person out. |
| Gas | thirdweb's bundler and paymaster for the thirdweb smart account (sponsorGas); our sponsor route is not involved for these operations. | No gas sponsoring for thirdweb-account transactions. The Safe route still sponsors (ours). | thirdweb could stop sponsoring: an interruption. |
| Bundler | thirdweb's hosted bundler for the thirdweb smart account; the proposal bundler goes through our `/api/bundler` proxy. | Sending stops for these accounts until another bundler is wired in. | An interruption. |
| RPC | thirdweb's RPC for the thirdweb account; PublicNode for passkey operations. | Reads stop until another RPC is wired. | Wrong reads, or logging of queries. |
| App distribution | The Apple and Google stores; Expo (EAS Update). Same as the other paths. | As on the other paths. | As on the other paths. |
| Database | Supabase, same as the other paths. | Same as the other paths. | Same as the other paths. |
| Hosting | Vercel and Fly, same as the other paths. | Same as the other paths. | Same as the other paths. |

## What no party can do

For an independent account (the passkey path with the passkey Safe, and the 12 Wörter path; the third path only after "Unabhängig werden"): none of the parties above can move or seize the account. The account is a Safe on Gnosis; moving anything needs a signature by the owner or a completed guardian recovery that the owner did not cancel in 3 days. Every party above can interrupt service (gas, bundler, RPC, sign-in on the passkey domain, install, profiles, backup, warnings). The honest limits: a lost keychain or a lost phone without words, file or guardians loses the account; and we cannot recover it.

## What we may say in the app

Each allowed claim is tied to a row. A claim not on this list is not made until a row supports it.

1. "Wir können dein Konto nicht bewegen." (independent accounts) - Key custody and "What no party can do".
2. "Dein Konto liegt auf der Blockchain, nicht auf unseren Servern." - Database and Hosting rows (the account, Bürgerkarte and Münzen stay on Gnosis).
3. "Mit den 12 Wörtern kannst du dein Konto in jedem Wallet öffnen." (12 Wörter) - Key custody, `docs/ACCOUNT_BACKUP_FILE_FORMAT.md`.
4. "Die Backup-Datei kannst du auch ohne die App öffnen." - Key custody on the 12 Wörter path; the format and script are in the repo.
5. "Wiederherstellungswörter starten eine Wiederherstellung; sie dauert 3 Tage, in dieser Zeit wirst du gewarnt und kannst sie abbrechen." - Recovery row (warnings are not guaranteed: email, push and banner).
6. "Ohne Apple, ohne Google, ohne Vertrauenspersonen" (a way back with only the words) - Recovery row. NOT allowed: "ohne uns" while gas, bundler and warnings run through us.
7. Never: "unabhängig" for the Google, Apple or E-Mail path before "Unabhängig werden" (Key custody row, third table).
8. Never: "Nur du hast den Schlüssel" for the passkey path while the keychain provider holds a synced copy of the passkey (Key custody row, passkey table).
